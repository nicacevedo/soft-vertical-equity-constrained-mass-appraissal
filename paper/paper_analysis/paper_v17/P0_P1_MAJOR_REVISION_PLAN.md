# P0/P1 Major-Revision Plan

## Target paper after revision

**Positioning:** a CCAO-centered applied-methods and diagnostic paper about training-time control of an observed-sale, price-related vertical-equity pattern in a research translation of the CCAO residential LightGBM workflow. It is **not** a general fairness solution, a tax-incidence paper, a universal penalty-selection paper, or evidence of production deployment.

**Primary empirical question:** Can a small, auditable change to the training objective attenuate the CCAO observed-sale log-ratio/log-price pattern while retaining the predictive advantages of the nonlinear learner, and what limitations appear as regularization strengthens and time moves forward?

**Primary contributions:**
1. Translate a classical log-log vertical-equity relationship into a training-time regularization target in a CCAO-motivated LightGBM workflow.
2. Derive and distinguish the Direct sample-coupled squared-covariance objective from the observation-additive Jensen Surrogate, including their fixed-prediction-space geometries.
3. Audit complete rolling-origin, held-out, and forward fixed-base-learner paths, emphasizing both improvements and failure modes rather than selecting a penalty.

**Secondary contributions:** Bayes residual-covariance caution; normalized penalty coordinate; nonlinear-shape diagnostic; complete-path infrastructure; reproducibility/audit machinery; secondary multi-jurisdiction stress-test infrastructure.

## Final main-body story after the revision

1. **CCAO baseline tension.** In a controlled research translation, LightGBM materially improves predictive performance relative to a historically motivated linear benchmark but exhibits a stronger observed-sale price-related ratio pattern.
2. **Training-time translation.** The classical log-log vertical-equity slope can be expressed as a covariance of log valuation ratio and log sale price. Penalizing it provides a one-parameter train-time intervention.
3. **Two distinct mechanisms.** Direct targets one signed sample-wide covariance and has dense rank-one curvature; the empirical tree implementation is Direct-diagonal. The Jensen Surrogate is exactly observation-weighted squared error and acts symmetrically on price tails.
4. **Complete temporal paths.** Rolling-origin CV, held-out evaluation, and 2025 show where first-order/assessor-facing diagnostics improve and where prediction, level, or ratio shape deteriorate.
5. **Failure-mode lesson.** Near-zero first-order slope is not a fairness certificate: nonlinear conditional-mean structure, broader dependence, local heterogeneity, and sale-selection limitations can remain.
6. **Scientific boundary.** No deployment penalty is selected. No general fairness or final-tax claim is made. In-processing superiority is not claimed until the centered-spread comparator is complete.

## Final publication claim if all TODOs pass

> In a CCAO-centered research workflow, covariance-guided regularization provides an auditable way to trace and attenuate a specified observed-sale price-related valuation-ratio pattern while retaining much of the predictive performance of the nonlinear learner over part of the path. The Direct and Surrogate objectives achieve this through distinct mechanisms and exhibit different failure modes. The exercise supports training-time control as one diagnostic/model-design tool, not a universal fairness solution or an automatically selected operating rule.

## Downgrade rule if a P0 experiment weakens the current descriptive result

The paper remains viable if the parity-correct rerun or centered-spread comparator erases the apparent predictive advantage. In that case, the empirical conclusion should become:

> The main value of covariance-guided in-processing is diagnostic and mechanistic rather than dominance: it exposes how a global first-order equity target changes the valuation path, how a simple post-hoc spread transformation captures much of the same first-order effect, and how the observation-weighted Surrogate can create nonlinear shape distortions despite apparently neutral slopes.

This downgrade preserves the paper’s scientifically strongest contribution rather than forcing a favorable numerical result.
