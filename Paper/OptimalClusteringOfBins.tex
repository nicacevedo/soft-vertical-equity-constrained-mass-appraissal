\section{Motivation}

The idea is to be able to create bins that are representative of analogous to the discrete labels in classification when $y \in \R^n$ is continuous (i.e., regression tasks). The motivation idea is that if we slice the range of $y$ with slices that are small enough, we get a good discretization of the variable. 

The novel approach is that we are not simply discretizing with uniform bins the range of $y$, but we are selecting bins that are the most representative of as some subgroups of the data $X\in\R^{n\times p}$, i.e., we are defining bins that are the best possible clusters when constrained to be in some bin defined by the possible options in the range of $y$. 

\section{Approach}

Let $R(y)=[L, U] \subseteq \R$ be the range of target $y$. For a given $K\in [n]$, we aim to select the best possible $K$-clusters. We further define an extra cluster $K+1$ for all the noise/outliers present in $X$. The formulation is then, with $M\gg0$ and $\rho >0$, 

{\everymath{\displaystyle}
\begin{align*}
    \begin{array}{rll}
 \min_{\ell, u, d, z, o} & \sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{i<j} d_{ij}^k + \rho \sum_{i=1}^n o_i\\
   s.t. & \ell_k \leq y_i + M(1-z_{i}^k),& \forall k \in [K], \forall i \in [n]\\
   & u_k \geq y_i - M(1-z_{i}^k),& \forall k \in [K], \forall i \in [n] \\
    & d_{ij}^k \leq z_i^k M,\\
    & d_{ij}^k \leq z_j^k M, \\
    & \norm{x_i - x_j} \leq \sum_{k=1}^K d_{ij}^k + \norm{x_i - x_j}  (o_i + o_j), & \forall i<j\\
    & \sum_{k=1}^{K} z_{i}^k + o_i = 1,& \forall i \in [n]\\
    &  \sum_{i=1}^{n} z_{i}^k \geq 2,& \forall k \in [K]\\
    & \ell_{i-1} \leq \ell_{i}, & \forall i \in [n] \\
    & u_{i+1} \geq u_{i}, & \forall i \in [n] \\
    & u_{i} \geq l_{i}, & \forall i \in [n] \\
    & \ell_0 = L\\
    & u_{K+1} = U\\
    & d_{ij}^k=0 & \forall i\geq j, \forall k \in [K].
    \end{array}
\end{align*}
}



\begin{align*}
\min_{\ell,u,\mu,z,o,d}\quad
& \sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{k=1}^K d_{ik} + \rho \sum_{i=1}^n o_i \\[2mm]
\text{s.t.}\quad
& \sum_{k=1}^K z_i^k + o_i = 1, && \forall i \in [n] \\
& \sum_{i=1}^n z_i^k \ge m_{\min}, && \forall k \in [K] \\[1mm]
& y_i \ge \ell_k - M_y(1-z_i^k), && \forall i,k \\
& y_i \le u_k + M_y(1-z_i^k), && \forall i,k \\[1mm]
& L \le \ell_k \le u_k \le U, && \forall k \\
& u_k \le \ell_{k+1}, && k=1,\dots,K-1 \\[1mm]
& \underline{x}_m \le \mu_{km} \le \overline{x}_m, && \forall k,\; m \in [p] \\[1mm]
& \|x_i - \mu_k\|_2 \le d_{ik} + M_x(1-z_i^k), && \forall i,k \\
& 0 \le d_{ik} \le M_x z_i^k, && \forall i,k \\[1mm]
& z_i^k \in \{0,1\},\ o_i \in \{0,1\}, && \forall i,k.
\end{align*}
