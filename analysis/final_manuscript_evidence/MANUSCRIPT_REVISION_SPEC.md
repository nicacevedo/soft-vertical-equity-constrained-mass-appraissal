## 0. Reference-cell semantics — the rule that governs the two path tables

A and C are **two frozen roles, not a substitution**. This is the single most consequential item in this document, and the fix is *not* to replace A with C.

| cell | forward display name | frozen role | comparison purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Ordinary LightGBM (standard raw-label native) | assessor-facing / workflow benchmark | `WORKFLOW_BENCHMARK` |
| **B** | Centered-label native L2 (initialization-aligned) | implementation-decomposition control only | `WITHIN_MODEL` |
| **C** | Custom-objective rho=0 origin | PRIMARY within-path penalty-isolating reference | `PENALTY_ISOLATING` |

The frozen attribution rules, quoted:

- **A** — comparisons against A are descriptively valid, but changes relative to A must NOT be attributed solely to rho
- **C** — C(rho=0) -> Direct/Surrogate(rho>0) is the clean penalty contrast and is the only comparison from which a rho effect may be attributed

Legacy Stage-1 label strings (`Parity-aligned native L2` for B, `Custom rho=0 origin` for C) may appear **only as values inside Stage-1 tables**, never as manuscript-facing display names.

### 0.1 Mandatory table highlighting and labelling convention

`tab:path_anchor_summary`, `tab:path_anchor_complementary`, `tab:ccao_baseline_results` and `tab:ccao_baseline_complementary` currently bold and star every penalized row against **Cell A alone**. The paper's own §5.1 caveat says native-to-penalized contrasts are confounded, and the frozen convention makes Cell C the only penalty-isolating reference. So the present highlighting reads as a penalty effect while resting on a workflow-benchmark comparison.

Required in the final tables:

1. **Keep** the A-referenced comparison. It is the assessor-facing workflow-benchmark question — *could a practitioner do better than the standard pipeline?* — and it is legitimately reportable, descriptively.
2. **Add** the C-referenced comparison and mark it as the **primary** one. It is the penalty-isolating question — *what does rho itself do?*
3. Use **two visibly different devices**, and never one device for both. A workable split: boldface for the workflow-benchmark comparison against Ordinary LightGBM, and a separate marked column, panel or superscript for the penalty-isolating comparison against the custom-objective rho=0 origin.
4. Say which is which **in the notes**, in words, naming both reference cells by their forward display names.
5. State, in the notes, that boldface and asterisks denote direction of movement only — never statistical significance, formal compliance, or model selection.
6. Never present an A-referenced difference as a pure penalty effect, and never label Cell C a workflow benchmark.

A row-level guard enforces the machine-readable half of this: no map row may carry `reference_cell=A` together with `comparison_purpose=PENALTY_ISOLATING`, and none may carry `reference_cell=C` with `comparison_purpose=WORKFLOW_BENCHMARK`.
